⚙ Design Constraints — encoded, not preached
01One active build-lane. All four divisions capture ideas freely; only one is "building" at a time. Contains the room-to-room pattern at the system level without asking you to stop generating.
02The substrate is Brobot's, not a separate app. This is your "one aggregate source." InterSpace becomes a view onto it later — earned, not led with.
03Self-improvement = bounded feedback loop. Reweight sources, tune prompts, log outcomes. You hold the judgment node. No agent rewrites its own architecture unattended — that fails silently and you can't catch the drift.
04Every stone is useful the day it lands. Stone #1 ingests newsletters and answers "what mattered this week" with zero UI. You benefit before the finish line — the whole point.
